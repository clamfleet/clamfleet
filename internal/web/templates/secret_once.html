Verify the signature

Every request carries the headers webhook-id, webhook-timestamp and webhook-signature (the Standard Webhooks format). The signature is v1, plus the base64 of the HMAC-SHA256 of id.timestamp.body. The HMAC key is the base64-decoded part after whsec_, not the whsec_ text. Reject timestamps more than 5 minutes away and remember seen ids for at least 2 hours (retries arrive with the same id). See docs/alerts.md for a verification example.
